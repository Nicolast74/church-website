not experimental features.

FEATURE ACTIVATION
--------------------------------------------------

1. JADWAL IBADAH & KEGIATAN (ENHANCED)

  - Ibadah Rutin

2. BACAAN HARIAN & IBADAH (ENHANCED)

UX GOAL:
Reading feels calm and focused, not like a raw text dump.

--------------------------------------------------

3. KALENDER LITURGI (ENHANCED)

- Monthly calendar with clear day blocks

Calendar is informative without being visually overwhelming.

4. GALERI KEGIATAN (ENHANCED)

Gallery Behavior:
- Card-based grid layout
- Uses real image data
- Organized by event or date (simple grouping)

Gallery Card:
- Image as primary focus
- Caption includes:
  - Event name
  - Date (optional)

UX GOAL:
Gallery communicates church life, not just photos.

5. HALAMAN LOKASI (ENHANCED)

Visitors understand each church location easily.
